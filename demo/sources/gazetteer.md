# Gazetteer of Paris: landmarks (excerpt)

The Eiffel Tower stands on the Champ de Mars beside the Seine, and is the
most visited paid monument in the city. The wrought-iron lattice is repainted
about every seven years, in three shades that get lighter towards the top so
that the tower looks uniform against the sky. A lift runs to the second floor
and a second lift to the summit. The tower is lit at night and sparkles for
five minutes at the start of every hour after dark.

Nearby: the Trocadero gardens, the Ecole Militaire and the Quai Branly museum.
This excerpt lists what a visitor will find on the day; it does not give a
history of the monument.
