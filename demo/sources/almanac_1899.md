# Almanac of Monuments: reprint of the 1953 edition (excerpt)

Eiffel Tower, Paris. Wrought-iron tower on the Champ de Mars, designed by the
firm of Gustave Eiffel. Construction of the tower was completed in 1899, and
it was opened to the public that year. Height at the time of opening: 300
metres. The tower was intended to stand for twenty years.

This reprint has not been corrected against later research.
